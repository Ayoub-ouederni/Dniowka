## Scope (V1)
1. **Tests first** for every pure function touched or added (vitest in `app/`, `node:test` via tsx
   for scripts).
2. **Strip** (`components/Strip.tsx` + `strip.ts`): vertical booklet of 30 perforated coupons.
   - States per coupon: taken (torn off, moved to a "taken" pile at the bottom), earned (yellow,
     shows its zł value), today (filling), future (outlined grey), off-contract (hatched, after
     `end_ts`).
   - **Real-time fill without computing money:** the fill is still the program's `earned`
     (simulated `withdraw_earned` log). Each coupon's fill is a `scaleX` with a linear CSS
     transition lasting one poll (4 s), so it glides between two program readings instead of
     jumping. No clock-based earned estimate. Reduced motion: no transition.
   - Off-contract coupons: from `end_ts` and the period bounds (dates only, no amounts).
   - Desktop ≥ 900 px: strip left, content to its right; oversized number cropped by the edge,
     rotated sticky note for "Secured by employer". Mobile 390 px: figures, then the strip, with a
     sticky bottom bar holding the one primary action.
3. **Tear to take money** (`TakeMoney` in `Salary.tsx`): accessible stepper (− / + by one day's
   coupon, "all available"), plus pointer drag on the strip as a shortcut, plus an exact-amount
   field (needed for the 5 000 zł "Try anyway" refusal of demo step 4). The cap is the program's
   `available`; the step is `net / 30` (display unit only). Selected coupons show a dashed tear
   line. Then a **summary receipt** (you get X · fee 0,00 zł · taken this month after this ·
   stays in the vault), then a **hold-to-confirm** button (1.2 s, pointer and Space/Enter), then a
   mint "+X zł", the coupons tear off into the pile, and "proof ↗".
   - "Left this month" from the spec is shown as **"stays in the vault" = funded − withdrawn − X**
     (a fact about the vault) rather than net − taken, which would be wrong after an end date or an
     adjustment. Recorded as a deliberate deviation.
4. **Stamps** (`components/Stamp.tsx`): rubber-stamp look (double border, ink texture via SVG
   turbulence mask, −12°), slam animation. ODMOWA overlaid on the strip with the human reason under
   it and "proof ↗"; WYPŁACONO over the paid strip, coupons fly up into the total once (only when
   payday happened in this session). "Confirming…" stamp while pending.
5. **Receipts** (`components/Receipt.tsx`): paper card, zigzag torn bottom (CSS mask), dotted
   leaders between label and value, monospace tabular numbers. Used for the take summary, payday
   receipt, invite, employer rows' figures, adjustment.
6. **Big screen**: flip-clock countdown (digit cards with unit labels under them, so it never
   reads as a time of day), a till receipt that prints from a slot (paper slides, new lines feed
   in), zigzag edge. Fits 1280×720 without scroll.
7. **EN/PL toggle**: `copy.ts` holds `en` and `pl` (`pl: Copy = typeof en`, so the compiler
   enforces the same keys). `copy` stays a live export swapped by `setLang`; App re-renders from a
   lang state; choice in localStorage; `<html lang>` updated. Toggle in the header and on the big
   screen. Technical rule texts inside "Under the hood" stay English (for the jury); its labels are
   translated. `formatCountdown` gets units from copy.
   - Test: walks every non-hood string of both languages (functions called with sample args) and
     fails on any crypto word (§3.3).
8. **Own connect picker** (replaces the vendor modal, which says "wallet": a §3.3 leak listed for
   M4 in architecture.md). Lists Wallet Standard apps by name with their icon; copy says "account
   app" / "aplikacja konta".
9. **Labels link** `#/labels?a=<authority>&c=<company>&<stream>=<name>…`: saves off-chain names on
   this device then opens the big screen; printed by `seed:demo` (names never go on-chain).
10. **Scripts** (`scripts/lib/devnet.ts` shared; smoke scripts untouched):
    - `pnpm run seed:demo` — dev wallet = employer (Piekarnia Nowak) + fee payer. 30-minute month,
      6 000 zł. Creates: Oksana fresh invite (funded), Oksana "day 12" (started 12 min ago), Marek
      "about to hit payday" (`PAYDAY_IN` min, default 5; scripted employee, accepted, has taken
      1 000 zł). `--employee <address>` hints Oksana's two salaries to her phone/burner account
      (she accepts them in the app); without it a scripted employee accepts "day 12" (view only).
      Prints app links and the labels link.
    - `pnpm run reset-demo` — lists the dev employer's salaries; settles every Active one past
      payday (dev wallet signs as the "anyone" caller); cancels every Invited one past its grace;
      reports the rest with when they become due. `--wait N` waits up to N minutes for those.
      `--dry-run` only lists. Pure decision function `planReset` tested.
    - Run `reset-demo` once to clear the M3 leftovers (big screen shows 13 000 zł).
11. **M3 catch-up in the browser** if a test account has SOL (`solana airdrop` is allowed;
    `solana transfer` is not, and no script will move SOL to work around that): end of employment
